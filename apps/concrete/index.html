The physics and what is learned
limit(T) = 90 min · 2^(−(T − 30) / 10)The 90-minute rule, with setting about twice as fast for every +10 °C.
slack = limit(T) − (now − batch time)Minutes of life left in each truck. The bar on every truck.
dn/dt = trucks arriving − trucks pumpedThe queue at the site obeys conservation.
next = argmin slack (among loads that can still be saved)Least slack first at the pump.
μ̂ = 1 / mean(last 6 unload times)Learned on site: the pump's real speed. Send the next truck only if it will still have slack when its turn comes.
