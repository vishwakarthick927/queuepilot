The physics and what is learned
t_drive = distance / speedDrive time through city traffic.
dn/dt = arrivals − c · μPatients in an emergency room: c doctors, each treating μ per minute (an M/M/c queue).
W_h = max(0, n_h − c_h + 1) / (c_h · μ_h)Expected wait at hospital h, counting ambulances already on the way.
h* = argmin_h ( t_drive + W_h )Go where treatment starts soonest.
μ̂_h = 1 / mean(last 12 treatment times at h)Learned tonight: each hospital's real speed. One hospital is short-staffed and slower than its plan.
